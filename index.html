PLAYER DEVELOPMENT

Potential Calculator

Estimate hidden potential from recorded training.

Player details

For ages 16–29, record each completed training week. You can change the primary and secondary skill every week.

How to enter training: Select the skill trained and enter its displayed value before and after the week. If it did not increase, enter the same number in both boxes (for example, 35 → 35). Do not leave either box blank. Secondary training is optional. If omitted, hidden fractions for other skills are reset because their training is unknown. Record every week in order for best results.

Training history

Experimental model: age and high-skill curves are interpolated estimates from guide anchors. Ages 16–29 only; enter age at each training tick if it changed. Omit injury-skipped weeks only by marking them as skipped. Results are candidate estimates, not exact potential. Consecutive observations of the same skill share rounding history even when its training role changes. Unobserved weeks break that history. Not a confirmed game-engine result.

03 / PLAYER DEVELOPMENT · BETA

Training Optimizer

Plan individual training or develop your entire roster. All forecasts are estimates.

Model assumptions & limitations

Uses the same interpolated age and skill efficiency curves as the Potential Calculator, eight training weeks per season, and fractional skill accumulation. Unknown starting fractions are treated as zero. Assumes no injuries. Forecasts stop at age 30; veteran decline is not modeled. PS is an estimated weighted skill score, not a guaranteed displayed game rating. Specialist weights are NEC estimates. The planner compares all 56 distinct pairs each week and improves the schedule using a bounded look-ahead; global optimality is not guaranteed. Potential scenarios follow the same recommended schedule.
