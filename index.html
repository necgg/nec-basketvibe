Hidden potential — experimental

For ages 16–18, record each completed training week. You can change the primary and secondary skill every week.

How to enter training: Select the skill trained and enter its displayed value before and after the week. If it did not increase, enter the same number in both boxes (for example, 35 → 35). Do not leave either box blank. Secondary training is optional.

Experimental model: fixed weekly gains and floor rounding, using the existing multipliers. All recorded skills must be below 70 and the age tier must remain unchanged. Consecutive observations of the same skill share rounding history even when its training role changes. Unobserved weeks break that history. Not a confirmed game-engine result.
